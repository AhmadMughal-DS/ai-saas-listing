Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Summary: ToolverAI is a directory and comparison platform. We collect minimal data needed to operate the service. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ToolverAI, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you create an account via Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication).
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Your email address if you choose to subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Tool submissions: Information you provide when submitting a tool for review (tool name, website, description, contact email).
  • Usage data: Pages visited, tools viewed, and interactions tracked via Google Analytics 4 (anonymized).
  • Technical data: Browser type, IP address (anonymized), and device type collected by our hosting infrastructure.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the ToolverAI directory and comparison features.
  • To send you our weekly AI newsletter (only if you subscribed).
  • To process and review AI tool submissions.
  • To analyze traffic patterns and improve user experience via Google Analytics 4.
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use ToolverAI. GA4 uses cookies and collects anonymized usage data. You can opt out of GA4 tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels beyond GA4.

4. Third-Party Services

ToolverAI integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase / Google: Authentication and data storage.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Database hosting for tool listings and user data.
  • Google Analytics 4: Usage analytics (anonymized).
  • Unsplash: Placeholder images for tool listings.

Each of these services has their own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party AI tools listed in our directory.

5. Affiliate Links & Disclosure

Some links on ToolverAI to AI tool websites may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and purchase a product, we may receive a commission. This does not affect our editorial rankings or reviews. See our Affiliate Disclosure for full details.

6. Data Retention

We retain newsletter subscriber data for as long as your subscription is active. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send. Account data is retained until account deletion is requested.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@toolverai.com

8. Children's Privacy

ToolverAI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of ToolverAI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@toolverai.com

You can also use the ToolverAI platform to submit a tool or reach us via social media at @toolverai.
