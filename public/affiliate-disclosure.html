Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Our commitment: ToolverAI maintains editorial independence. Our rankings, comparisons, and reviews are based on real data — not affiliate relationships.

FTC Compliance Statement

In compliance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), ToolverAI discloses that we may receive compensation when visitors click affiliate links and make purchases.

What Are Affiliate Links?

Some links on ToolverAI to AI tool websites may be affiliate links. This means if you click a link marked as an affiliate link and purchase a subscription or product, ToolverAI may receive a commission from the AI tool provider.

Affiliate links are identified where applicable. The presence of an affiliate relationship does not add any cost to you — you pay the same price regardless of whether you use our link.

How Affiliate Relationships Affect Our Content

We want to be completely transparent:

  • Rankings are based on estimated traffic data — monthly visit statistics sourced from publicly available web analytics estimates. Commercial relationships do not influence ranking positions.
  • Reviews reflect real user feedback — user-submitted reviews on ToolverAI are not influenced by commercial relationships.
  • Deals are curated and checked — promotional deals listed on ToolverAI are checked prior to publication. We list deals regardless of commercial affiliation.
  • Category placements are not sold — tools are categorized by their actual function, not by commercial arrangement.

Sponsored or Featured Listings

ToolverAI may offer "Featured" listing placements to AI tool companies. Featured listings are clearly marked with a "Featured" badge. Featured placement represents a commercial relationship and is not an editorial recommendation. Our rankings are separate from featured placement.

Outbound Links

When you click "Visit Website" or "Get Deal" buttons on ToolverAI, you may be redirected via a tracking link. This allows us to monitor click-through rates and in some cases earn affiliate commissions. This tracking does not collect your personal information beyond what is standard for web analytics.
