Editorial Policy & Standards

By ToolverAI Team • Last Updated: October 2026

ToolverAI aims to provide clear, organized software discovery for artificial intelligence tools. This policy explains how we collect product information, create guides, use AI assistance, and handle corrections.

1. Information Sourcing & Aggregation

Directory listings, comparison tables, and guides on ToolverAI are assembled using publicly accessible sources:

  • Vendor Websites & Documentation: Product descriptions, feature lists, pricing plans, and platform support details are gathered directly from vendor homepages, pricing pages, and public documentation.
  • Estimated Traffic Data: Web visit metrics displayed across the platform represent third-party estimates from public analytics and web telemetry sources. They serve as broad adoption indicators, not precise first-party user counts.
  • Community Reviews: Star ratings and reviews are contributed by registered users. We do not independently verify user employment or claims.
  • Scope of Testing: We do not conduct exhaustive laboratory benchmark testing on every listed product. Readers are encouraged to test tools and confirm current pricing and enterprise terms directly on vendor websites.

2. Use of AI Assistance in Content Creation

To provide structured guides across a fast-changing industry, ToolverAI uses AI tools to help research, outline, and draft comparison summaries and educational articles. The ToolverAI team curates these outputs to ensure formatting consistency, clear categorization, and relevance to developers and teams.

3. Commercial Relationships & Placement

Organic directory listings and metric-based sorting (such as sorting by estimated monthly visits) are not sold. Any sponsored listing, featured placement, or partnership on ToolverAI is clearly marked (such as with a "Featured" or "Sponsored" tag). Commercial relationships do not alter organic sort results.

4. Affiliate Disclosures

Some outbound links to software vendors may be affiliate links. If a user clicks an affiliate link and makes a purchase, ToolverAI may receive a referral commission at no additional cost to the user. For complete details, see our Affiliate Disclosure.

5. Updates & Corrections

Software features, pricing tiers, and domain addresses change frequently. We review correction requests as promptly as practical. If you notice an outdated price, incorrect feature, or broken link, please let us know:

  • Contact Email: marketing@toolverai.com
  • Please include the tool name, URL, and the specific correction needed.
